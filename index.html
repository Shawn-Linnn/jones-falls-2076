    Speculative vision

    Speculative visions from the Jones Falls 2076 project, which gathers many ideas rather than proposing one plan. This scene is not an official plan.

    Design concept by [to be confirmed]

    Real-world scale in meters, but the valley is shortened along its length. The 1850s, 1915, and today scenes combine documented details with generalized layout; they are not traced from historical photos or maps.

    The Baltimore skyline in the background changes with each period and uses real building heights, but it is placed behind the default view for composition. Geographically, downtown lies south of this stretch of the river.

    Sources: Woodberry Factory (Baltimore Heritage), The Jones Falls (Baltimore Heritage), The Baltimore Banner, 1912 conduit description, Penn-Fallsway, AARoads I-83.

    Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan. Arrow keys step through time.

    Sky switches the time of day and the weather for every period: morning, golden hour, overcast, rain (wet streets and puddles) and night (street lamps and lit windows).

    Walk puts you at street level as a small figure: walk with W A S D or the arrow keys (or the joystick on a touch screen), drag to look around, and click or tap the ground to jump there. Switching the period while walking keeps you on the same spot.
